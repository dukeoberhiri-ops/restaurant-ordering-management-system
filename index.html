Our story

Built around one fire, not a menu of gadgets.

Ember & Salt opened in 2014 with a single oak hearth and a short list of ideas: cook honestly, source from the coast, and let the fire do most of the seasoning. Everything since has grown from that hearth outward.

No fryers, no microwaves, no shortcuts — just embers, cast iron, and a kitchen that closes when the coals do.

Meet the chef

Chef Marisol Reyes

Raised in a fishing town, trained in wood-fire kitchens up and down the coast, Marisol runs the hearth the way her grandmother ran a beach bonfire — by feel, not by timer.

Yes, delivery is available within 5 miles. Choose "Delivery" at checkout on the menu page.
Is there a dress code? +
Smart casual. We just ask no beachwear after 5 PM.

Every dish lists ingredients and allergens on the menu page — let your server know of anything serious.
